Jim Cortez is a San Francisco-based artist behind Electric Gerbil, creating immersive installations at the intersection of engineering and art. His work is designed to spark connection between strangers through responsive light, playful interaction, and shared ritual.

---

![Jim Cortez in studio](/images/jim_artist_profile.jpg)

---

Electric Gerbil projects combine engineered LED systems, custom hardware control, and story-driven environments that invite participation. From deep-playa dining installations to evolving public works, the goal is consistent: build spaces where curiosity, whimsy, and inclusion create memorable human moments.

## Practice

- Immersive and interactive light sculpture
- Site-specific installations and participatory environments
- Real-time media systems spanning embedded devices and show control software
- Collaboration with camps, fabricators, and artist communities
